What worked, what didn't

  • Worked: the chain as phases. Clean → rack → front squat → drive → press → lower → return reads as one breath. Planted feet + dropping the pelvis gives a real front squat without a second stance. Side and front both teach; three-quarter is fine for a Library look.
  • Worked: floor-hold at the start. Without it the hanging rack bell punched through the floor. Per-phase discrete hold: "floor" (not on the cubic) keeps the bell on the ground until the clean. Reaching a true floor handle forces a deeper hinge than the app's stick-figure start — closer to a crouch.
  • Didn't: one arm-pole for rack and press. Elbow bend is still a per-movement constant. Rack wants the elbow out and forward; lockout wants it under the bell. The compromise pole looks soft at both ends. The clean path also needed a hip-height waypoint or the cubic folds the forearm through the thigh.

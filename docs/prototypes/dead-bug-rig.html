What worked, what didn't

  • Worked: the side view. The held leg stays at 90/90 because its foot is a fixed target, the other leg extends to a hover, the bell travels on an arc overhead and stops just above the floor, and the second rep is a mirror.
  • Didn't: the front view. From the feet at eye level the raised shins stack on the torso and the bell. I raised the camera to nearly overhead to separate them and it still reads worse than the side. For this movement the side view does the teaching.
  • Didn't: the low-back cue. The spine is one rigid segment, so "low back glued down" can't be shown. The bell's arc also needed a middle phase; with only start and end, the elbows bend halfway.

What one rep stores


      
The bell handle and the moving foot, five phases. The body lying down, the held leg, the knee and elbow solving, the hanging bell and the left-leg mirror are fixed values or shared code.
